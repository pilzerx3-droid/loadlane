The information exists, but it is scattered across emails, calls, and systems. Loadlane puts it on one screen, so your team can focus on the decisions only people can make.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can Loadlane connect to SAP or Salesforce?

Yes, that is the plan. At launch you can connect any system through the open API and webhooks, email-in, or CSV import. Salesforce, Zapier, and EDI status messages come next. SAP connections are set up with your IT team or an integration partner.

Do drivers need to install an app?

No. Drivers get a link on their phone and tap to report each step.

How do documents get attached?

Forward an email to the load’s address, upload through the API, or run a script. You can also add files by hand.

What does the dashboard show?

Every active load with its status and ETA, a timeline and live position per load, all documents, and alerts for anything that needs attention. The pictures on this page show the planned dashboard with sample data.
